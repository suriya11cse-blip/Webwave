WEBWAVE - About Us
==================
WEBWAVE is a smart platform that provides real-world opportunities, industry-oriented courses, and skill development programs. We help students and job seekers connect with local opportunities, internships, part-time jobs, and career guidance. All services are 100% free for job seekers.

WEBWAVE Core Service Domains
=============================
1. Real-World Opportunities: Internship Programs, Part-Time Job Support, Career Mentoring & Counselling.
2. Demand-Driven Digital Skills: Content Creation and Canva Designing.
3. Career & Professional Development: Practical skill-building and industry-oriented opportunities.
4. Advanced Technologies: AI, Robotics & Drone Basics.
5. Medical Coding Course.
